          What is measured

          Orbital imagery and terrain elevation. Slope, path lengths and ascent are derived from those grids. Targets are proposed, not rover or astronaut locations.

          What is modeled

          Archived Mars Climate Database context for one location and season. It is not live weather or a site-specific forecast.

          What still needs evidence

          Radiation dose, current storms, sub-grid rocks, suit performance and verified surface resources. The prototype does not certify a safe Marswalk.

          Global context, local science

          Explore beyond Arcadia's outlined 10,000 km² sector. Detailed science routing stays within the loaded 4.8 × 4 km HiRISE inset. Jezero also supports routing inside its loaded 6 x 5 km terrain footprint.

          THE EXPLORERS ? VERSION 1.0

          Your Marswalk, planned with evidence.

          1. Choose Arcadia or Jezero. Enter a starting place and destination, or place the pins on the map.
          2. Set your walking speed and slope limit in the left panel, then press Get directions.
          3. Compare the shortest route with the route that minimizes the maximum slope. Editing a setting clears the routes until you press the button again.
          4. In Arcadia, switch to 3D to inspect measured terrain. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The relief multiplier exaggerates height.
          5. Open Marswalk to arrange science stops, set mission limits, and save or export your briefing.

          Measured terrain coverage is outlined. The 100 ? 100 km Arcadia border marks the regional dataset; you can explore beyond it.

          Research and team demonstration only. ?Safest? compares terrain slope within the available grid. It does not assess every hazard or certify astronaut safety. Atmospheric conditions are archived model samples, not live weather. Review Sources & Saved for provenance and limitations.
